Turn historical sales into practical purchasing decisions. Compare forecasting methods, see actual sales versus forecast, and export your results. Free demo: first 15 products.

1. Import sales data

Columns: COD, Description, STOCK, EXPECTED SKU, RESERVED SKU and at least 12 monthly sales columns such as -1 MONTHS SALES (most recent), -2 MONTHS SALES, etc.

Select 1–12 months. Seasonal forecasting is available with 24 or more months of history.

All processing takes place in your browser. No sales files are uploaded to TiedChain servers. The Excel library is loaded from an external CDN.
